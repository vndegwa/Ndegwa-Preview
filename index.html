I am Ndegwa, a student actuary with a focus on product development and pricing across life insurance, general insurance and banking and investment products. I grew up in the semi arid Rift Valley, where drought is a baseline condition, and that is where my interest in climate risk and pricing comes from.

  • Pricing and product development. Life insurance, Nairobi. Individual, retirement and group products, plus the tools behind them.
  • Assistant Editor in Chief, The Actuary East Africa. The Actuarial Society of Kenya's magazine, read in about 20 countries.
  • TASK Convention. Scientific and review committee, and member of the TASK ERM, Life and Reinsurance Working Party.
  • University of Cape Town. Actuarial graduate studies, with the MSc in Climate Change.
  • Milliman and ARC. ASP scholar.

Publications holds my research, gap analyses, pricing studies and regulatory comparisons. Products shows the life insurance products I have priced and developed. Projects covers what I am building outside the day job.
